# Celebration of Departure (for a decayed Angel)

A lifeless sunlight fills this cursed room,\
Where no life could ever dare to bloom;\
Inside, three people; whose souls are long gone,\
As holy chants hint at the end that is to come.

A bond shared through blood, memories and insanity;\
Tortures the three innocent prisoners in unity,\
For the distant memories of sephia hue comfort all;\
As they hold on to dear life of a decayed angel.

Oh, what a joy! That this merciful angel, once blessed;\
Departs from her rotten husk that as if it were possessed.\
Oh, what a shame! That a helpless sheep with a broken mind;\
Had to be cut through her flesh, strangled with a red bond.

A celebration for the departed! A solemn lament for living!\
As the heavy breath of life comes into their broken being.\
Weight of the tears not enough to bring their smiles down;\
For with their sanguine bond torn, they could finally live on!
