# Sinners in Blight

Black blight surrounds me under dim moonlight.\
My scorched throat makes no sound; yet I drown not.\
My torched eyes see naught; yet I feel a presence;\
Equally dark, equally damaged; I am held by a gentle cradle:

I make out the patches of jet black; bright and elaborate:\
Deep in the mire as I; yet floats with unseen radiance.\
Darker than the blight of sin; her eyes strip me of eclipse\
Weight removed from my shoulders; her beautiful hands meet mine.

An unfamiliar emotion fills us both; lighter than blight\
Our flesh set in motion; we embrace each other in flight\
We take our first breath in years above the dark lake;\
I haven't time to rejoice; her rejuvenating lips meet mine
