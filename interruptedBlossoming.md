# Interrupted Blossoming

White mountains surround the procession of death\
Of a bloated, sick heart under the black sky\
Long gone is the wolf; bereft of its teeth\
Stark gales push the dirt; as there's none to bury.

Not even petals of withered sunflowers present\
Atop the half grave of mine; only my rotten scent\
Winds howl and lament my departure ever stronger\
As dirt clenches to awaken me; comes a known stranger.

Dried leaves and white dust cover her slender darkness\
And with tears of white tracking the path of her sadness\
In her beautiful gaze, intent on interrupting my eternal sleep\
An ambiguous desire, the rotten pulp of flesh is hers to keep.

Her hand goes gently into the transparent flesh\
Where the lilies, poppies and roses were to bloom\
Prolonged now is my departure; a departure in a flash\
Alas, not for long; for I trust naught but my own doom.
